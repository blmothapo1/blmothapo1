How to Use Lux Layer

.lux-ambient

Adds soft ambient glow overlays. Apply to hero sections or large containers.

<section class="lux-ambient">...</section>

.lux-spotlight

Creates a cursor-tracking spotlight effect. Combine with .lux-ambient for best results.

<section class="lux-ambient lux-spotlight">...</section>

.lux-glow

Adds soft edge glow and hover lift effect. Perfect for cards, buttons, and CTAs.

<div class="lux-glow">...</div>

.lux-glass

Frosted glass effect with backdrop blur. Use for overlays or premium cards.

<div class="lux-glass">...</div>

.lux-sweep

Light sweep animation triggered on scroll. Automatically activated by lux.js.

<div class="lux-sweep">...</div>
